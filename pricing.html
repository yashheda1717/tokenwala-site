1. Plans

Guests never pay Tokenwala directly. Organizers pick a plan. All prices include 18% GST. Your payment gateway’s own fees (for example Razorpay’s) are separate and are charged by the gateway.

How the usage fee works. Volume is the total paid at your stalls and counters in a calendar month (IST). Only the part above your plan’s allowance is charged. Example: a Mela with ₹4,00,000 of payments in a month pays 0.5% of ₹1,00,000 = ₹500. The fee is invoiced at the end of the month — it is never taken from your guests’ money or your gateway settlements.

2. Guest convenience fee (optional)

You can add a convenience fee of up to ₹20 to each online top-up, to cover your costs. It is never more than 5% of the top-up (a ₹100 top-up carries at most ₹5).

  • Guests see the fee and the total before they pay, and on their receipt. It is never hidden or added at the last step.
  • The fee goes into your own gateway account with the top-up. Guests’ Tokens are not reduced.
  • Counter cash top-ups and kiosk top-ups carry no fee.
  • Tokenwala invoices you 50% of the fees collected each month. The fee is your charge to guests, so GST on it is yours to account for — ask your CA.
  • It is off by default. Turn it on, change it or turn it off in the event settings.

4. Sponsor slots

Sponsor slots let you earn from local businesses at your event: a sponsor’s art on guests’ passes, a line on receipts, or a banner on the top-up screen. You agree the price with your sponsor and keep all of it; Tokenwala charges only the add-on above (or nothing on Mela and Pro). Sponsors never get guests’ personal data — only totals. Sponsor content must follow the ASCI Code and may not promote betting, online money games, tobacco or alcohol.

5. Billing

  • Plans are paid on the web at tokenwala.co, not inside the iPhone or Android app.
  • Invoices are issued by Tekhnologia Innovations India Private Limited, GSTIN 27AAKCT6250G1Z5. Organizers that deduct TDS should share the TDS certificate each quarter.
  • Launch pricing may change. Paying organizers get at least 30 days’ notice, and a change applies from the next billing period or next event.
  • Cancellations and refunds: see our Cancellation and refund policy. Full terms: Organizer Agreement.
