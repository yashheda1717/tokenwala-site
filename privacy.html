1. Who we are and what this covers

Tokenwala (“Tokenwala”, “we”, “us”) is a software platform operated by Tekhnologia Innovations India Private Limited (CIN U58201MH2023PTC415332), registered office 2nd Floor, Wing B, 234/3 to 324/1, Risod Road, Patani Chowk, Washim, Maharashtra 444505, India. It lets event organizers and outlets (“Organizers”) run closed-loop digital tokens and prepaid passes for their own event or outlet, lets stalls and counters (“Stalls”) accept them, and lets guests (“Guests”, “you”) top up and pay.

This policy explains what personal data we process through the Tokenwala mobile apps, the web wallet, the organizer web app and this website (together, the “Services”), why, and the choices and rights you have. It is written with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the rules made under it, and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, in mind.

2. Our role and the Organizer’s role

Two kinds of party decide how your data is used:

  • Tokenwala as Data Fiduciary for your Tokenwala account: your phone number, name, profile photo, devices, security settings, sign-in records, and the operation, security and improvement of the platform.
  • The Organizer as Data Fiduciary for the event or outlet you join: your pass at that event, your top-ups, payments, refunds and any information the Organizer asks for. For this data Tokenwala acts as the Organizer’s Data Processor, processing it on their behalf and under their instructions, under a written agreement.

Payment gateways (such as Razorpay or Cashfree) that the Organizer connects process your payment details under their own privacy policies. Tokenwala never receives your full card number, UPI PIN or bank credentials.

4. Why we use it

  • To provide the Services: create your account, issue and run passes, process top-ups, payments and refunds, show balances and receipts, and let Stalls and Organizers see the payments made to them.
  • To keep money safe: verify approvals, enforce Quick Pay caps and PIN locks, detect unusual activity (for example many rejected payments or PIN failures), freeze lost phones and bands, and reconcile our ledger against gateway and cash records.
  • To meet legal duties: keep transaction records, respond to lawful requests from authorities, handle grievances and meet tax and accounting rules.
  • To communicate: one-time passwords, payment and refund notifications, low-balance and spend alerts you turn on, service messages, and, only if you opt in, WhatsApp receipts.
  • To improve the Services: fix bugs and understand aggregate usage. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.
  • To bill Organizers: work out plan usage, usage fees and convenience-fee shares from totals. Invoices to Organizers contain totals, not Guests’ personal data.

5. Consent and legitimate uses

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create an account and accept this policy, and which you may withdraw at any time (see Your rights). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already done, and we may need to keep some records afterwards where the law requires.

Some processing relies on the legitimate uses recognised by Section 7 of the DPDP Act — for example, where you voluntarily provide data for a specific purpose such as a top-up, or where we must comply with a law or court order.

6. Who we share it with

  • The Organizer of an event or outlet you join: your name, masked phone number, pass, top-ups, payments, refunds and any problem you report at that event.
  • Stalls you pay: your first name or pass label, masked pass number, amount, items and time.
  • Payment gateways connected by the Organizer (for example Razorpay, Cashfree) to take top-ups and process refunds.
  • Service providers who help us run the Services under contract, including cloud hosting and database (Supabase (Tokyo region)), SMS/OTP delivery, push notifications, email, and crash reporting (only if enabled). If you opt in: WhatsApp Business messaging, Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes.
  • Sponsors never receive your personal data. Where an Organizer shows sponsor messages, sponsors may see only totals, such as how many people saw a message.
  • Authorities where required by law, court order or to protect the rights, property or safety of users or the public.
  • A successor if Tokenwala is merged or acquired, subject to this policy.

We do not sell or rent personal data.

7. Where data is stored

Production data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in its Tokyo (Japan) region. Some other service providers (for example SMS delivery and payment gateways) may also process limited data outside India. Transfers outside India follow Section 16 of the DPDP Act, and we will stop or move any transfer to a country the Government of India restricts. Where Indian law requires records to be kept in India (for example, system logs under the CERT-In Directions of 28 April 2022), we keep them in India.

8. How long we keep it

  • Ledger and transaction records are kept for 8 years from the end of the financial year they relate to, as needed for refunds, disputes, reconciliation and the record-keeping periods of the Companies Act, 2013 and GST law, even after an account is deleted. The ledger is append-only, so corrections are recorded as new entries.
  • Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted or anonymised within 30 days of a verified deletion request, except what must be kept as above.
  • System and security logs are kept for 180 days, as the CERT-In Directions require, or longer if needed for an investigation. Rate-limit counters are kept for 90 days.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you and the processing activities (the app’s data export gives you a copy of your account and transactions);
  • Correct, complete or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erase your data, subject to the retention duties above (use “Delete account” in the app, or see how to delete your account without it);
  • Withdraw consent at any time, with the same ease with which you gave it;
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity;
  • Seek grievance redressal from us, and then, if unresolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

You may give, manage and withdraw consent through a Consent Manager registered with the Data Protection Board of India, once one supports Tokenwala.

For data an Organizer controls, we will pass your request to that Organizer and help them respond. Before deleting an account with a balance, please ask for a refund where the event allows it; balances on deleted accounts follow the Organizer’s leftover policy.

10. Children

Under the DPDP Act a child is anyone under 18. Children should use Tokenwala only through a family sub-pass created and managed by a parent or lawful guardian, or with an NFC band or cash card the guardian obtains. By creating a sub-pass, the guardian gives verifiable consent for the limited processing needed to run it (a label, limits, payments and alerts). We do not knowingly create accounts for children, track them, monitor their behaviour, or show them targeted advertising.

School fair cards. At a school fair the school (the Organizer) may hand out printed cards that carry only a number, a label such as “Fair 001”, a QR code and a PIN. We store no child’s name, class or roll number for these cards; the school keeps its own list. A parent or guardian may add a card to their own Tokenwala account by scanning it, typing its PIN and giving consent; we record who linked it, when, and the version of the consent text. The parent may add a nickname and sees the card’s balance and purchases. Removing the card from the account withdraws that consent; the card then keeps working anonymously with its PIN until the event closes.

11. Security

We use reasonable security safeguards, including encryption in transit, access control at the database row level, signed and time-limited payment codes, device-bound approval keys, hashed PINs, encrypted storage of gateway keys, rate limits, audit logs and nightly reconciliation. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will tell affected users without delay, notify the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Rules require (including a full report within 72 hours), and report cyber incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours.

12. Cookies and this website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies and loads no third-party analytics. The web wallet and organizer app store a sign-in session and preferences in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in; clearing site data signs you out.

13. Changes to this policy

We will post updates here and change the date at the top. For material changes we will notify you in the app before they take effect and, where the law requires, ask for your consent again.
