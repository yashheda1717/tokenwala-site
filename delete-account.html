Your data

Delete your Tokenwala account

You can delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time, with or without the app.

In the Tokenwala app

  1. Open Tokenwala and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. Under Your data, tap Delete account.
  3. Read what goes and what stays, type DELETE and confirm.

The account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on every phone.

Without the app

Email hello@tokenwala.co with the subject “Delete my Tokenwala account” and the mobile number you sign in with. To protect you, we first confirm the request with you on that number (call or WhatsApp), then delete the account within [30 days] and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number and profile photo;
  • Your phones, PIN, Face ID / fingerprint approval keys and Quick Pay settings;
  • Notifications, WhatsApp receipt settings and saved preferences.

What we keep, and why

  • Payment and ledger records, without your name, for the period tax, accounting and payment laws require ([e.g. 8 years]), so organizers, stalls and refunds still add up.
  • Records an organizer must keep for its own event (for example a refund it still owes you).

If a pass still has money on it

Use it, gift it, or ask for a refund where the event allows it before you delete your account. Balances left on deleted accounts follow the organizer’s leftover rule shown when you topped up. See the privacy policy for details.
