2. What Tokenwala is — and is not

  • Tokenwala is software. It lets an Organizer issue closed-loop tokens (“Tokens”, held on a “Pass”) for one event or outlet, and lets Guests use them to buy goods and services from that event or outlet only.
  • The Organizer is the issuer and the seller. Tokens are issued by the Organizer and are redeemable only for goods and services sold at its event or outlet — by the Organizer itself or by Stalls that supply them on the Organizer’s behalf under the Stall Agreement. Each Pass and receipt shows the Organizer’s name (“Issued by …”).
  • Tokens are not money, not a bank deposit and not a wallet usable elsewhere. They cannot be withdrawn as cash, transferred to a bank account, used at another event or outlet, or exchanged for anything other than goods and services at that event or outlet, except for refunds as described below. They earn no interest.
  • Tokenwala does not hold, pool, settle or transfer funds. Top-ups are paid directly to the Organizer’s own account with a payment gateway (such as Razorpay or Cashfree) or to the Organizer’s cash counter. Tokenwala is not a bank, a payment aggregator, an issuer of prepaid payment instruments or a marketplace, and it does not sell the goods or services bought with Tokens.
  • Tokenwala earns only from fees it invoices to Organizers (see section 10). It never deducts anything from Guest funds or from an Organizer’s gateway settlements.

5. Top-ups, payments and leftovers (Part B — Guests)

  • Top-ups. You buy Tokens for a specific event or outlet by paying the Organizer through its payment gateway, or in cash at its counter. A top-up is credited once the gateway confirms it; failed or uncaptured payments are reversed by the gateway under its own timelines.
  • Convenience fee. An Organizer may add a convenience fee to online top-ups (at most ₹20, and never more than 5% of the top-up). If it does, the fee and the total you will pay are shown on the top-up screen before you pay, and on your receipt. The fee is the Organizer’s charge, is paid to the Organizer’s gateway account, and is not added to your Tokens. Counter cash top-ups carry no fee. If a payment is captured but cannot be credited (for example, the event has closed), the whole amount, fee included, is refunded automatically. Otherwise the fee is not refunded when unused Tokens are refunded, unless the event is cancelled or the law requires it.
  • Bonuses and program credit. Top-up bonuses, loyalty credit, offers and employer program credit are promotional, may expire and may be non-refundable, as shown when they are given.
  • Payments. You pay a Stall by scanning its QR, letting it scan yours, or tapping a band or card. Payments above the limit set by the Organizer need your PIN or biometric approval. A payment is final when you see the receipt, except that a Stall may undo it within 15 minutes, and except for refunds below.
  • Quick Pay. If you switch on Quick Pay, small payments up to the cap you choose (and the Organizer allows) go through without approval for the time you choose. It switches off automatically in some situations, such as a new device. You accept the risk of payments made while it is on.
  • Offline payments. When a Stall is offline, it may accept payments up to per-Pass caps; these are recorded and synced later and count as normal payments.
  • Family passes, transfers and gifts. You may move Tokens between your own family passes at the same event and, where the Organizer allows, send a capped gift to another Guest at the same event. Tokens never leave the event or outlet.
  • Leftover balance. Each event or outlet has a leftover policy set by the Organizer and shown before you top up: refund to the original payment source (newest top-ups first; cash top-ups at the counter), donate to a named cause, forfeit, or for outlets keep until expiry. Where an outlet allows refunds on request, you may request one at most once a day. Refunds to UPI or card are started by the Organizer’s gateway, usually within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • Problems and disputes. Use “Report a problem” on the transaction. Disputes about goods, services, prices or quality are between you and the Organizer (who answers for its Stalls); Tokenwala will share the transaction record and help where it can. Please raise a dispute with the Organizer before raising a chargeback with your bank.
