2. What Tokenwala is — and is not

  • Tokenwala is software. It lets an Organizer issue closed-loop tokens (“Tokens”, held on a “Pass”) for one event or outlet, and lets Guests use them to buy goods and services from that event’s or outlet’s participating Stalls and counters only.
  • Tokens are not money, not a bank deposit and not a wallet usable elsewhere. They cannot be withdrawn as cash, transferred to a bank account, used at another event or outlet, or exchanged for anything other than goods and services at that event or outlet, except for refunds as described below. They earn no interest.
  • Tokenwala does not hold, pool, settle or transfer funds. Top-ups are paid directly to the Organizer’s own account with a payment gateway (such as Razorpay or Cashfree) or to the Organizer’s cash counter. The Organizer is the issuer of its Tokens and is responsible for the funds it receives.
  • Tokenwala is not a bank, payment aggregator or issuer of prepaid payment instruments. [Counsel to confirm the regulatory characterisation under the RBI Master Directions on Prepaid Payment Instruments and the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007, and any conditions to be added here.]

  • You must be 18 or older to hold an account. Children may use the Services only through a family sub-pass, band or card managed by a parent or lawful guardian.
  • You must give accurate information and not impersonate anyone.

5. Top-ups, payments and leftovers (Part B — Guests)

  • Top-ups. You buy Tokens for a specific event or outlet by paying the Organizer through its payment gateway, or in cash at its counter. Gateway charges, if any, are shown before you pay. A top-up is credited once the gateway confirms it; failed or uncaptured payments are reversed by the gateway under its own timelines.
  • Bonuses and program credit. Top-up bonuses, loyalty credit, offers and employer program credit are promotional, may expire and may be non-refundable, as shown when they are given.
  • Payments. You pay a Stall by scanning its QR, letting it scan yours, or tapping a band or card. Payments above the limit set by the Organizer need your PIN or biometric approval. A payment is final when you see the receipt, except that a Stall may undo it within 15 minutes, and except for refunds below.
  • Quick Pay. If you switch on Quick Pay, small payments up to the cap you choose (and the Organizer allows) go through without approval for the time you choose. It switches off automatically in some situations, such as a new device. You accept the risk of payments made while it is on.
  • Offline payments. When a Stall is offline, it may accept payments up to per-Pass caps; these are recorded and synced later and count as normal payments.
  • Leftover balance. Each event or outlet has a leftover policy set by the Organizer and shown before you top up: refund to the original payment source (newest top-ups first; cash top-ups at the counter), donate to a named cause, forfeit, or for outlets keep until expiry. Where an outlet allows refunds on request, you may request one at most once a day. Refund timelines depend on the gateway and your bank, usually [5–7 working days].
  • Problems and disputes. Use “Report a problem” on the transaction. Disputes about goods, services, prices or quality are between you and the Stall or Organizer; Tokenwala will share the transaction record and help where it can. You should raise a dispute with the Organizer before raising a chargeback with your bank.

  • Use undo (within 15 minutes) only to correct genuine mistakes. Keep your stall phones, staff access and pay QR safe; revoke lost phones from the dashboard.
  • Settlement is made by the Organizer from its own account, based on the settlement statement the Services prepare. Tokenwala does not pay Stalls and is not liable for an Organizer’s failure to pay.
  • You are responsible for your own licences, food safety, GST and other taxes on your sales.
