Cancellation and refund policy

How refunds work for guests, organizer plans, fees and add-ons. The full rules are in our Terms of use; if the two differ, the Terms apply.

1. Guest top-ups at an event or outlet

Money you top up is received by the event Organizer or outlet (in their own payment gateway account or at their cash counter), not by Tokenwala. Tokens can only be spent on goods and services at that event or outlet.

Unused balance. Every event or outlet shows its leftover policy before you top up. It is one of: refund to the original payment source when the event closes (newest top-ups first; cash top-ups are refunded at the counter), donation to a named cause, forfeit, or (for outlets) kept until the pass expires. Where an outlet allows refunds on request, you can ask for one in the app at most once a day.

Timelines. Refunds to UPI or card are started by the Organizer’s payment gateway, usually within 5–7 working days of the event closing or your request, depending on your bank.

Failed or late payments. If money is taken but your Tokens are not added (for example, because the event has just closed), the full amount — including any convenience fee — is refunded automatically to where it came from.

Convenience fee. If the Organizer charges a convenience fee on online top-ups, it is shown before you pay. It is refunded with a failed or late payment, or if the event is cancelled. It is not refunded when unused Tokens are refunded at the end of an event.

Event cancelled. If an Organizer cancels an event, it must refund all unused balances and convenience fees to the original payment sources, whatever leftover policy it had announced.

Promotional credit. Top-up bonuses, offers, loyalty and program credit may expire and may not be refundable, as shown when they are given.

2. Payments at a stall

A payment is final once you see the receipt. A stall can undo a payment within 15 minutes (for example, a wrong amount); the amount goes back to your pass. For a problem with goods or service, use “Report a problem” on the transaction in the app. It goes to the Organizer, who decides on a refund.

3. Organizer plans

  • Per-event plans (Mela) are charged once per event. You can cancel for a full refund until the event goes live, or within 7 days of paying if it has not gone live by then. After the event goes live, the fee is not refunded.
  • Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, with no further charges. A yearly plan cancelled within 14 days of first purchase is refunded in full if no event has gone live on it.
  • Trials are free and end on their own; nothing is charged unless you choose a plan.
  • Apart from the above, fees already paid are not refunded, except where the law requires it or where we fail to provide the Services through our fault. In that case we refund the fees for the affected period.

4. Usage fees and convenience-fee shares

These are worked out from your actual monthly volume and invoiced at the end of the month. If an invoice is wrong (for example, a refunded payment was counted), tell us within 30 days and we will issue a credit note.

5. Add-ons

  • Digital add-ons (extra stalls or kiosks, WhatsApp packs, branding, sponsor slots) can be cancelled for a full refund until they are first used. Unused WhatsApp messages are not refunded once a pack has started.
  • NFC wristbands and printed QR cards are made to order. Orders can be cancelled for a full refund before production starts (we confirm by email). Items that arrive damaged or faulty are replaced free if you tell us within 7 days of delivery, with photos.
  • On-ground setup and event-day support: cancelled 7 or more days before the event, full refund; 2–7 days before, 50% refund; less than 48 hours before, no refund. Travel already booked is charged at cost.

6. Delivery of physical items

NFC wristbands and printed QR cards are shipped by courier within India, usually within 7–10 working days of the order being confirmed and paid. Shipping charges, if any, are shown on the order. You will get a tracking link by email or SMS. Goods and services bought with Tokens at an event are supplied by the Organizer and its stalls, not shipped by Tokenwala.

7. How to ask for help

Guests: use “Report a problem” on the transaction in the app first. Otherwise, or for organizer billing and add-ons, write to hello@tokenwala.co or call +91 80072 05205 with your phone number, the event and the transaction. We acknowledge requests within 24 hours. Approved refunds of Tokenwala’s own fees are paid to the original payment method within 7 working days.
