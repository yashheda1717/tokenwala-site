Cancellation and refund policy

This page summarises how refunds work on Tokenwala. The full rules are in our Terms of use; if the two differ, the Terms apply.

1. Guest top-ups at an event or outlet

Money you top up is received by the event Organizer or outlet (in their own payment gateway account or at their cash counter), not by Tokenwala. Tokens can only be spent on goods and services at that event or outlet.

Unused balance. Every event or outlet shows its leftover policy before you top up. It is one of: refund to the original payment source when the event closes (newest top-ups first; cash top-ups are refunded at the counter), donation to a named cause, forfeit, or (for outlets) kept until the pass expires. Where an outlet allows refunds on request, you can ask for one in the app at most once a day.

Timelines. Refunds to UPI or card are started by the Organizer's payment gateway, usually within 5–7 working days of the event closing or your request, depending on your bank. A payment captured after an event has closed is refunded in full automatically.

Promotional credit. Top-up bonuses, offers, loyalty and program credit may expire and may not be refundable, as shown when they are given.

2. Payments at a stall

A payment is final once you see the receipt. A stall can undo a payment within 15 minutes (for example, a wrong amount); the amount goes back to your pass. For a problem with goods or service, use “Report a problem” on the transaction in the app. It goes to the Organizer, who decides on a refund.

3. Organizer plans and fees

Per-event plans are charged once per event. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel; you can cancel at any time and the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, with no further charges. Fees already paid are not refunded, except where required by law or where we fail to provide the Services through our fault.

4. Products and delivery

Tokenwala is software. We do not ship physical goods. Goods and services bought with tokens are supplied by the stalls at the event or outlet.

5. How to ask for help

Guests: use “Report a problem” on the transaction in the app first. Otherwise, or for organizer billing, write to hello@tokenwala.co with your phone number, the event and the transaction. We acknowledge requests within 48 hours.
