Stall agreement

The terms between an organizer and each stall or counter that accepts Tokenwala tokens.

Version 1.1 · effective 21 November 2026 (1.1: tabs)

1. What this is

This Stall Agreement is between the Organizer of an event or outlet on Tokenwala and the business or person running a stall or counter there (the “Stall”). The Stall accepts it when it joins the event in the Tokenwala app; the Organizer accepts it by approving the Stall. The Organizer may use its own written agreement instead, if it includes at least these terms. Tekhnologia Innovations India Private Limited (“Tokenwala”) provides the software and is not a party to the commercial terms between Organizer and Stall.

2. The Stall sells on the Organizer’s behalf

  • At the event, the Stall supplies goods and services to Guests as the Organizer’s vendor or concessionaire. Guests pay with Tokens the Organizer issued; the Organizer pays the Stall afterwards.
  • The Stall accepts Tokens only through the Tokenwala app, QR, band or card, and only for goods and services it actually supplies at that event or outlet.
  • The Stall must never give cash, change or any other value in exchange for Tokens, sell Tokens, or let anyone use the Stall’s QR outside the event.

3. Prices and conduct

  • The Stall charges only its displayed price (in the app menu or at the stall), including any taxes, for genuine goods and services.
  • Undo is for genuine mistakes only, within 15 minutes. The Stall helps the Organizer resolve Guests’ complaints within 15 days.
  • The Stall keeps its phones, staff logins and pay QR safe, and revokes lost phones at once.
  • The Stall holds the licences its business needs, including FSSAI registration or licence for food, and follows food-safety, weights-and-measures and other applicable rules. It sells nothing unlawful, and no tobacco or alcohol where not permitted.

4. Commission and settlement

  • The Organizer and Stall agree the commission rate (shown in the app), any stall rent, and the settlement schedule before the event.
  • The Organizer pays the Stall the payments it received in Tokens, less the agreed commission, refunds and undos, within the agreed schedule — or within 7 days of the event closing if none was agreed — by bank transfer or UPI from the Organizer’s own account.
  • The settlement statement prepared by the Services is the starting point. Either side raises any difference within 7 days of receiving it.
  • Tokenwala does not pay Stalls, holds no money for them, and is not responsible for any amount the Organizer owes.
  • Tabs (khata). A tab is the Stall’s own interest-free credit to a customer it knows; the Stall decides who and how much, carries any unpaid amount itself, and settles with the customer directly. No interest, late fees or EMIs. Tokenwala only keeps the record.

5. Taxes and records

Each party is responsible for its own taxes. The Organizer and Stall agree with their own advisers who is the supplier of each sale for GST, and the Stall issues any bill it must. The Organizer’s commission is the Organizer’s service to the Stall and may carry GST if the Organizer is registered. Both keep the records the law requires.

6. Data

The Stall uses Guests’ details it sees in the app (a first name or pass label, masked pass number, items and amounts) only to serve that Guest, and not for marketing or any other purpose. The Organizer is the Data Fiduciary for event data and Tokenwala processes it for the Organizer. The Stall’s own details are handled under the Tokenwala Privacy policy.

7. Ending and disputes

  • The Organizer may pause or remove a Stall for breach of this agreement, fraud or a Guest-safety risk. Settlement for payments already made still follows section 4.
  • This agreement ends when the event closes and settlement is complete, or for an outlet when either side gives 15 days’ notice.
  • It is governed by the laws of India. Disputes go first to the Organizer; if not settled in 30 days, to the courts having jurisdiction over the event’s location, unless the Organizer and Stall agree otherwise in writing.
