1. Parties and acceptance

This Organizer Agreement (“Agreement”) is between Tekhnologia Innovations India Private Limited (CIN U58201MH2023PTC415332, GSTIN 27AAKCT6250G1Z5), 2nd Floor, Wing B, 234/3 to 324/1, Risod Road, Patani Chowk, Washim, Maharashtra 444505 (“Tokenwala”), and the business, society, school, college or other body that creates an organization in the Tokenwala organizer app (the “Organizer”, “you”). You accept it when you create an organization or start a plan. The person accepting confirms they may bind the Organizer. Our Terms of use also apply; this Agreement prevails for the matters it covers.

2. What Tokenwala provides

Tokenwala grants you a non-exclusive, non-transferable right, for the term of your plan, to use the Tokenwala apps, organizer web app, kiosks, offline tools, dashboards and reports (the “Services”) to run closed-loop token events and outlet passes, within your plan’s limits and any add-ons you buy. Tokenwala provides software only. It does not take part in your sales, hold your money, or supply the goods and services at your event.

3. You are the issuer and seller of record

  • You issue the Tokens for each of your events and outlets. Guests buy Tokens from you and spend them only on goods and services sold at your event or outlet.
  • Stalls act for you. Each stall at a multi-stall event supplies goods and services on your behalf as your vendor or concessionaire, under the Stall Agreement or your own written agreement containing at least the same terms. You answer to Guests for what they buy at your event, and settle with stalls afterwards. This keeps Tokens as an instrument for buying from you alone.
  • Tokens may not be withdrawn as cash, sent to a bank account, used outside the event or outlet, or offered as a general-purpose wallet. Transfers are limited to a Guest’s own family passes and capped gifts within the same event.
  • You will not market Tokens as money, a wallet, an investment or a stored-value product usable elsewhere.
  • You confirm you are not operating a marketplace in which Tokens pay unrelated third-party sellers outside these arrangements. If that changes, you must tell us first: such an arrangement may need an RBI-authorised prepaid payment instrument issuer, and we may suspend Tokens for it until it is resolved.

4. Your money and your gateway

  • You connect your own payment-gateway merchant account (for example Razorpay or Cashfree), after completing its KYC, using your own keys. Guest top-ups and convenience fees go into that account; cash top-ups go into your cash drawer. Tokenwala never receives, pools, holds or settles your money and is not a payment aggregator.
  • You keep your gateway account in good standing, follow its terms, and bear its fees, chargebacks and disputes.
  • You pay stalls from your own account, based on the settlement statements the Services prepare. Tokenwala is not liable for any amount you owe a stall or a Guest.
  • Your gateway keys are stored encrypted and are never shown back in full. Only owners can connect or change a gateway.
  • Tabs (khata). If you or a Stall turn on Tabs, any goods given on a tab are your (or that Stall’s) own interest-free credit to a customer you know. You decide who gets a tab and its limit. Tokenwala only keeps the record and sends reminders; it does not lend, assess credit, guarantee or collect any amount, and bears no loss. You must not charge interest, late fees or EMIs through Tabs, and you settle tabs directly with the customer by cash or your own UPI.

5. Your duties to Guests

  • Before a Guest tops up, the Services show your name and contact details, the leftover policy, refund timelines, pass validity and any convenience fee. You must keep these accurate and not change the leftover policy to Guests’ detriment after they have topped up.
  • New events default to Refund of leftover balances. If you choose Forfeit, you must announce it clearly at the venue as well as in the app. If you choose Donate, you must name the cause and pass the money on.
  • If you cancel an event, you must refund all unused balances and any convenience fees to the original payment sources, whatever leftover policy you announced.
  • You handle Guests’ complaints about goods, services and refunds promptly, and in any case within 15 days. You follow the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and the Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023 — including no hidden charges, no pre-ticked add-ons and no false urgency.

6. Guest convenience fee

  • You may set a convenience fee of up to ₹20 per online top-up. The Services cap it at 5% of the top-up and show it, with the total, before the Guest pays and on the receipt. You may not charge any other fee to Guests for topping up or paying through Tokenwala, at the venue or elsewhere.
  • The fee is your charge for your service to Guests. You are responsible for GST and other taxes on it. Tokenwala invoices you 50% of the fees collected each month (see section 9).
  • Failed and late-captured payments are refunded in full, fee included, automatically. On event cancellation you refund the fee too.

7. Lucky draws

  • Draws are off by default. If you switch one on, you are its sole sponsor and promoter. You confirm you hold any permission your state or local authority requires, and you are responsible for the prizes and for tax on them (including TDS on prizes above ₹10,000 under the Income-tax Act).
  • Every draw must offer free entries to all Pass holders (no purchase necessary), must not offer cash or cash-equivalent prizes, and must follow the official rules shown in the app. You may not run a draw, contest or game that is an online money game under the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025.
  • Apple and Google are not sponsors of any draw and must not be named as such.

8. Sponsor slots

  • Sponsor slots are included in the Mela and Pro plans and available as an add-on on other plans. You agree terms with your sponsors and keep what they pay you.
  • You are responsible for sponsor content. It must be lawful, follow the ASCI Code, be truthful, be shown as sponsored, and not promote betting, online money games, tobacco, alcohol, or anything unlawful or unsafe for children. We may remove content that breaks these rules.
  • You will not give sponsors Guests’ personal data from the Services. Sponsors may be given totals only.

9. Plans, fees, invoices and taxes

  • Plans are bought on the web at tokenwala.co at the prices on our pricing page. All prices include GST at 18%. Per-event plans cover one event; monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until cancelled.
  • Usage fee. For each calendar month (IST), Tokenwala invoices the plan’s usage-fee percentage on payment volume above the plan’s monthly allowance.
  • Convenience-fee share. 50% of the convenience fees your Guests paid in the month.
  • Add-ons are invoiced when ordered or, for usage-based add-ons, monthly.
  • Invoices are payable within 15 days. Late amounts may carry interest at 1% a month, and we may pause new events (never live ones or refunds) while an invoice is more than 30 days overdue, after 7 days’ notice.
  • Fees are never deducted from Guest funds or your gateway settlements and are never collected by splitting a Guest’s payment.
  • If you deduct TDS, give us the certificate each quarter. Price changes need 30 days’ notice and apply from your next billing period or event. Refunds of fees follow our Cancellation and refund policy.

10. Acceptable use

You will not use the Services: to sell goods or services that are unlawful at your venue; for cash-out, lending, remittance, betting, gambling or online money games; to evade KYC, tax or anti-money-laundering rules; to collect Guests’ data for purposes unrelated to your event; or in a way that harms the Services or other users. You are responsible for your team members’ actions. You will keep permissions, licences and insurance your event needs.

11. Data processing

For personal data of Guests and Stalls at your events and outlets, you are the Data Fiduciary and Tokenwala is your Data Processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025. For users’ Tokenwala accounts (phone number, profile, devices, security) Tokenwala is the Data Fiduciary.

  • Tokenwala processes event data only to provide the Services, on your documented instructions (your settings and requests), and keeps it confidential. Its staff are bound by confidentiality.
  • Tokenwala uses reasonable security safeguards (encryption in transit, row-level access control, encrypted keys, audit logs, backups) and keeps system logs for 180 days.
  • Sub-processors: Supabase (database and functions, Tokyo region), Twilio (SMS one-time passwords), your payment gateway (Razorpay or Cashfree), and, where enabled, Meta (WhatsApp), Apple and Google (Wallet passes, push notifications) and crash reporting. We will tell you before adding a sub-processor that receives event data.
  • Tokenwala will tell you without undue delay, and within 24 hours where it can, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your event data, and help you notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected people. Tokenwala reports cyber incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours.
  • Tokenwala helps you answer Guests’ requests to access, correct or erase data, and handle grievances.
  • When your plan ends, you can export your reports for 30 days. Event data is then deleted or anonymised, except records the law requires to be kept (such as transaction records for 8 years).
  • You confirm you have a lawful basis for any data you add (for example, staff phone numbers) and that you will not ask Guests for more data than the event needs.

12. Intellectual property and confidentiality

Tokenwala owns the Services and its brand. You keep your names, logos, event content and data, and give Tokenwala a licence to use them to run the Services. You may let us name you as a customer unless you tell us not to. Each party keeps the other’s non-public business information confidential, except as the law requires.

13. Warranties and liability

  • Tokenwala will provide the Services with reasonable skill and care. Otherwise, to the extent the law allows, the Services are provided as is. Networks, phones and gateways can fail, so keep counter cash as a fallback for large events.
  • Each party’s total liability under this Agreement in any 12 months is limited to the fees you paid Tokenwala in those 12 months. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss or lost profits. These limits do not apply to your payment obligations, your indemnity, breach of confidentiality, or fraud.
  • You indemnify Tokenwala against claims by Guests, stalls, sponsors or authorities arising from your events, goods, services, draws, sponsor content, taxes, permissions or breach of this Agreement.

14. Term, suspension and ending

  • This Agreement lasts while you have an organization on Tokenwala. You may close your organization after all events are closed and leftover policies have run.
  • We may suspend Services for an event, after notice where practical, to stop fraud, a security risk, a legal breach, or misuse of Tokens. We will always let refunds and settlement statements run.
  • Either party may end this Agreement for material breach not fixed within 15 days of notice. Sections 4, 9, 11, 12, 13 and 15 survive.

15. Law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes not settled within 30 days of notice will be decided by a sole arbitrator appointed jointly under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated at Washim, Maharashtra, in English. Either party may seek urgent interim relief from the courts at Washim, which otherwise have exclusive jurisdiction.

Notices: to Tokenwala at hello@tokenwala.co; to you at the owner’s phone number or email in the organizer app.
